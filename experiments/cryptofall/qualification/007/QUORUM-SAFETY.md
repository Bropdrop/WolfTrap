# Quorum safety and limits

**Agreement is conditional.** The fixed membership consists of four replicas. At most one identity over the lifetime of a configuration may sign inconsistently, lose its durable lock, be restored to an older volume, or have its signing key copied. A process crash with storage intact does not consume this fault budget. A compromised signer can send arbitrary statements. Delays, partitions, lost responses and repeated delivery may last indefinitely. Identities are authenticated, keys are assumed unforgeable, and clocks used for authorization are trusted wall clocks. A malicious supervisor of all four local processes lies outside this model.

Two sets of three drawn from four intersect in at least two members. With at most one faulty identity, at least one intersection member is honest. Its unique durable height lock forbids signing different blocks at that height, including after restart. Therefore two conflicting certificates at a first divergent height are impossible under those assumptions. Induction over the checked parent digest gives prefix agreement. Two-of-four has disjoint quorums and fails this argument. The design has no view-change or lock-release mechanism. It provides neither unconditional progress nor a general Byzantine fault-tolerance qualification.

Each accepted effect checks the unspent permit and unused request against its unique predecessor. It writes both terminal mappings with the one-unit increment and exact receipt. A second execution in a later certified state fails the spend check. Any apparent second execution on a divergent branch requires a violation of the agreement assumptions. The relay has no independent physical increment to repeat. Copying databases or summing the four cached values would miscount effects.

Revocations, replacement and effects compete in the same certified order. A quorum that already voted an effect precludes a competing same-height revocation. A winning revocation precludes completion of a conflicting effect and changes what the next height can authorize. An honest vote validates the claim at its recorded issuance time. Old votes can be disclosed after expiration, but an expired claim cannot obtain a new honest vote. A certificate does not prove wall-clock network delivery or disk flush occurred before expiration. The experiment did not qualify clock drift, faulty clocks, cryptographic breaks or real-time deadlines.

Safety of commitment and availability of proof are distinct. Three surviving accessible votes suffice to reconstruct a certificate even if no installed copy survived. Losing access to evidence can leave a committed request UNRESOLVED to the caller. A 2–2 durable split can prevent further progress forever. An authenticated but malformed response can withhold progress. There is no automatic unlock, dynamic membership, signer refresh or proof of global absence.

Voting keys confer no owner, witness, execution-gate or permit authority. The public verifier rechecks the prior policy and full command. Even a fixture-generated three-signature certificate for an increment without owner approval failed review. The independent floor detects coordinated local rollback when the presented chain omits or conflicts with the retained block. Presenting conflicting certificates directly also freezes review. If enough signers and all independently retained history are compromised, a reviewer seeing only one internally consistent alternate chain may accept it. Secure erasure, mobile compromise, independent key custody and hardware monotonic state were not qualified.

## Local custody and transport

The experiment runs four OS processes with separate SQLite volumes, replica signing seeds and per-relay/per-replica link secrets, plus fresh relay processes. RPC over loopback TCP uses nonce-bound HMAC-SHA-256 challenge and response authentication. Partitioned paths are redirected to a TCP endpoint that closes the connection. Each accessible path still reaches the real replica. QUORUM used one Unix account and one supervising process that generated all test keys. Same-host storage, clock and administrator compromise are common-mode risks. The transport is authenticated but has no confidentiality layer. The bounded lab uses small histories and the predecessor's capped RPC frames. Long-chain synchronization, reconfiguration, encrypted wide-area transport and independent administration need separate qualification.

The disposable ledger has a single audit domain. Provisioned relays and offline reviewers can read full certified histories, including other operations on the same counter. Exact disclosure checks bind the returned terminal decision to the presented operation, evidence and permit. Selective disclosure to mutually distrusting clients was outside this qualification.

The partition experiment covers asymmetric reachability from each proposing relay. Replicas do not run a peer election or gossip protocol. Relays carry proposals and certificates between them. Honest replica identities must never have simultaneous writers with independent copies of a vote database. The permit issuer, policy roots, execution gate and claim signers are trusted within their respective roles. An honest owner signature proves what that key endorsed. It does not establish genuine human approval in this fixture.
