# WT-008 protocol and safety assumptions, fixed before implementation

## Mechanism and scope

Use a fixed-membership, two-phase prepared-certificate view change derived from the single-slot rule in Castro and Liskov, Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance, OSDI 1999, §4.4. Each member signs its own promise and its own prepare and commit votes. Any authenticated relay may assemble a proposal. Removing the distinguished primary permits competing proposals and weakens progress. This is a narrow local protocol, with a new genesis and signature domain. WT-007 ballots have no WT-008 meaning. There is no migration of live locks or production state.

## State and transitions

For each ledger, height and certified parent, each replica persists promised view (initially zero), the last prepared certificate on which it issued a commit vote, one prepare and one commit ballot per view, and its signed view reports. Transactions serialize an entire operation. Respond only after database commit. Genesis, membership and application authority are independently provisioned. Prepare and commit sign distinct domains covering genesis, height, parent, numeric view, exact block digest, time and member identity. Prepare certificates and commit certificates each require three distinct valid members in the same phase and view.

1. Promise(v). An authenticated relay requests a strictly higher view. Atomically promise to cease all fresh lower-view votes and sign a report for v with the highest locally commit-voted prepared certificate or null. A retry for the current view returns its original report. A timeout alone carries no authority.
2. Prepare(v,b). At local promised view v, validate application authority and the block against the current parent. View zero uses no reports. Every higher view requires three authenticated promises for precisely this slot and view. Verify every enclosed prepare certificate from a strictly lower view. If any exist, b must match the unique block digest at the greatest reported prepared view. Otherwise any authorized b may be proposed. Sign at most one prepare per view. Coordinators may carry different eligible promise sets.
3. Commit vote(v,b). Verify a prepare certificate for precisely this view, slot and block and require the local promise still equals v. Revalidate current application authority and time. Persist the prepared certificate and commit ballot atomically. Three commit ballots decide the block, even if no coordinator saw the last response. An old prepare certificate can constrain a later proposal, but cannot authorize fresh later-view commit votes.
4. Install. Verify an exact commit certificate and independently replay the application transition at each voting time. Atomically record history and successor including counter, request, consumed permit and receipt. Delayed final certificates remain valid at their original height and parent. Repeated installation is idempotent. A conflicting certified history or failure to extend the retained floor freezes review.
5. Recover. Use the original operation, approvals and signed disposable permit to identify the exact terminal receipt. Recover historical votes without reauthorizing the effect. Otherwise return UNRESOLVED. A certified fence proves NOT_COMMITTED. An empty response cannot prove absence.
6. Governance. Preserve WT-007 ownership, witness, gate, policy and dual-authority recovery rules. In WT-008 the signed permit ID commits to its unique lease and absolute expiry. Enforce that deadline separately from the 30-second maximum claim lifetime at every fresh effect ballot. A safely carried value may expire and stall. Revocations compete as certified ledger transitions. A view change cannot override them.

## Agreement and progress

Assume four pinned replica identities, at most one cumulatively Byzantine (including rollback, copied keys or unsafe concurrent storage writers), intact non-rollback vote storage on honest nodes, unforgeable signatures, independently retained review checkpoints, and trusted honest clocks and role keys. Single-host tests share an administrator. A compromised administrative majority or loss of every independent pin falls outside the guarantee.

Distinct three-member quorums intersect in at least two members. At least one is honest. Two different prepare certificates in one view would require an honest double prepare. Once a value has three commit signatures, at least two honest committers hold its prepare certificate. Every subsequent three-report quorum intersects them in an honest member. That member committed before issuing its higher promise or cannot participate in the old commit quorum. By induction, a higher prepared certificate reported in its place must carry the same value. Thus no later view can prepare or decide a conflicting value. Prefix agreement follows by the certified-parent check. Application replay separately enforces authorization and at-most-once spending.

Progress requires a reachable cooperative quorum, compatible promises, an available eligible payload, live authorization and eventual coordination around one proposal. Arbitrary partitions, incessant view churn, unavailable evidence or expired carried permits may stall indefinitely. There is no automatic pacemaker, membership change, administrative unlock, physical-clock deadline on delivery, or blanket Byzantine fault-tolerance claim.

## Independent bounded model

Enumerate one height, two competing authorized block values, three honest identities plus a maximally equivocating fourth, asynchronous vote omissions, every three-report selection and every permissible carried prepare certificate, for consecutive bounded views. Distinct targets may use different promise quorums. Track honest commit locks, prepare certificates and decisions across views. Check uniqueness of preparation within each view and agreement across all decisions. Honest restart preserves state. Honest rollback counts as a fault. Cryptographic correctness, application validation, time and multilevel storage behavior are tested separately. This model establishes bounded safety, not liveness.
